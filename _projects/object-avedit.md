## Object-AVEdit: An Object-level Audio-Visual Editing Model

**Status:** Under Review for ICLR 2026  
**Role:** Co-first Author  
**Affiliation:** GeWu-Lab, Renmin University of China

### Overview

Object-AVEdit presents a novel approach for audio-visual editing at the object level, enabling precise manipulation of audio-visual content based on object-specific controls.

### Key Contributions

- Developed an object-level audio-visual editing framework
- Built on extensive research of diffusion-based generation and editing techniques
- Enables precise control over audio-visual content manipulation
