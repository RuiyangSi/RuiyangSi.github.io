## LLM Reasoning in Combinatorial Optimization

**Status:** Ongoing Research  
**Affiliation:** THU-C3I, Tsinghua University  
**Duration:** October 2025 - Present

### Overview

This research investigates the boundaries of Large Language Model (LLM) reasoning in combinatorial optimization problems, with the goal of enabling LLMs to tackle NP-hard problems while ensuring robust generalization across diverse domains.

### Research Goals

- Explore the capabilities and limitations of LLM reasoning in solving combinatorial optimization problems
- Develop methods to enable LLMs to handle NP-hard computational problems
- Ensure robust generalization across different problem domains
- Bridge the gap between natural language understanding and formal problem-solving

### Research Context

This work builds upon my background in competitive programming and problem-solving, applying structured algorithmic thinking to advance LLM reasoning capabilities. The research aligns with my interest in building general intelligent systems capable of accelerating scientific discovery through verifiable reasoning.
