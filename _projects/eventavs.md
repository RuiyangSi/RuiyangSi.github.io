## EventAVS: Aligning Audio and Video Generation Model with Event-level Synchrony

**Status:** Under Review  
**Affiliation:** GeWu-Lab, Renmin University of China

### Overview

EventAVS introduces a novel approach for aligning audio and video generation models with event-level synchrony, achieving more coherent and temporally consistent multimodal generation.

### Key Contributions

- Developed event-level synchronization mechanisms for audio-visual generation
- Enhanced temporal consistency in multimodal generation systems
- Advanced audio-visual co-generation techniques

### Authors

Youquan Fu, Song Huang, **Ruiyang Si**, Ping Luo, Jiacheng Sun, Hongyuan Zhang, Di Hu, Xuelong Li

### Research Context

This work contributes to tasks in audio-visual co-generation and complex audio editing based on Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs), conducted during my research internship at GeWu-Lab.

### Supervision

Supervised by **Prof. Di Hu** at the GeWu Lab, Renmin University of China (February 2025 - September 2025).
