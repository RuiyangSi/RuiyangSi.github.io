I am a third-year undergraduate student majoring in Computer Science and Technology at BUPT. My research interests are centered on GenAI, with a primary focus on Unified Models and Large Language Model (LLM) Reasoning.

I am driven by the goal of building general intelligent systems capable of accelerating scientific discovery—systems that possess both verifiable reasoning and the ability to understand multimodal information. My experience as an algorithm competition participant and problem setter has given me a strong foundation in structured problem-solving, which I now apply to my research in LLM reasoning.

I am currently working as a research intern at THU-C3I (Tsinghua University), supervised by Prof. Ning Ding and Dr. Youbang Sun, investigating the boundaries of LLM reasoning in combinatorial optimization. Previously, I worked at GeWu-Lab (Renmin University of China) under Prof. Di Hu, focusing on audio-visual editing and multimodal generation.

I have been honored with the CCF Elite Collegiate Award (Top 99 nationwide) and the National Scholarship for two consecutive years (2024, 2025). I am also an active competitive programmer, having won multiple gold medals in ICPC and CCPC contests.
