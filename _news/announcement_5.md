Excited to share that our paper "Object-AVEdit: An Object-level Audio-Visual Editing Model" has been submitted to ICLR 2026!

This work proposes a novel approach for audio-visual editing at the object level, building on extensive research of diffusion-based generation and editing techniques. I'm honored to contribute as co-first author alongside Youquan Fu.

**Links:**

- [arXiv preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.00050)
- [Project Page](https://gewu-lab.github.io/Object_AVEdit-website/)

This research was conducted during my internship at GeWu-Lab, Renmin University of China, under the supervision of Prof. Di Hu.
